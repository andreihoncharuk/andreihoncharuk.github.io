Privacy policy — AI Slop: PvP Battle

Last updated: 27 November 2026

AI Slop: PvP Battle (“the app”) is published by Six Strokes Studio (Andrii Honcharuk), sixstrokesstudio@gmail.com. This policy covers the AI Slop: PvP Battle app for iPhone, iPad and Android (com.sixstrokes.aislop).

In short

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No account: you are never asked for your name, email or any login.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Only online play and the daily leaderboard use the internet.

What the app stores on your device

Your heroes, favourites and shelf, Slop Rating, coins, unlocked ammo and powers, stats, badges, daily challenges and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them. We hold no copy.

Online play and the daily leaderboard

Online matches and the daily leaderboard run on Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). When you use them, the app:

  • signs in anonymously through Unity Authentication, which creates a random player ID for this installation. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple or Google account or advertising identifier;
  • uses Unity Lobby and Relay to find and connect a match. A public lobby carries only the game's version;
  • sends the other players in your match your hero (its name and look) and what you do in the match;
  • after a match, submits your points for the day to the daily leaderboard (Unity Leaderboards) with your hero's name, which other players can see on the list.

Hero names go through a word filter before anyone else sees them. As with any internet connection, Unity receives your device's IP address while connecting. Unity processes this data on our behalf only to run these features; see Unity's privacy policy. LAN play connects devices on your own network directly and sends nothing to us or to Unity.

Sharing

The picture card of your hero is made on your device. It goes only where you send it, through your device's own share sheet.

Children

The app collects no personal information and has no chat. Players can only send one of a few fixed phrases to each other during a match.

Your choices

  • Play offline against bots, or over LAN, and nothing leaves your network.
  • To have your leaderboard entries removed, email us the hero name and the day; we will delete them.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored game data.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted at this address, with the date at the top changed to match.
