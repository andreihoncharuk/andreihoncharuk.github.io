Delete your data

Six Strokes Studio (Andrii Honcharuk) · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from Last Fire (com.sixstrokes.lastfire) and Hot Pursuit Zero (com.sixstrokes.hotpursuitzero).

Invite-code data (stored by us)

The only data we keep outside your device is the data behind the optional invite feature: an anonymous random ID for your installation, your invite code, and a record of which anonymous ID entered which code. To have it deleted:

  • Open the game and the invite window (Last Fire: the minigun button on the title screen; Hot Pursuit Zero: the Bulldozer in the garage or the button on the home screen) and note your code.
  • Email sixstrokesstudio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, the name of the game and your code.
  • We delete your code, the anonymous ID it belongs to and every redemption record linked to it within 30 days and reply when it’s done. Nothing is kept after that.

Deleting this data doesn’t take away a minigun or Bulldozer you already unlocked on your device, but the code stops working for new friends.

Game progress

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps, to delete them. We have no copy.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never receive your payment details. Your purchase history is managed in your Google account (Google Play → Payments & subscriptions).

Advertising data

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which acts as an independent controller. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and manage data Google holds at myaccount.google.com.
