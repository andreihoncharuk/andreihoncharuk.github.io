Privacy policy — Dead Field

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Dead Field (“the app”) is published by Six Strokes Studio (Andrii Honcharuk), sixstrokesstudio@gmail.com. This policy covers the Dead Field mobile app (Android package com.sixstrokes.deadfield).

What the app stores

Your run history (score, kills, time survived, threat level and date), the weapons and upgrades you own, your cells, and your sound settings are saved only on your device. The app has no account system and does not send your progress anywhere.

Uninstalling the app removes this data. Because none of it ever leaves your device, we hold no copy of it and there is nothing on our side to export or delete.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address, device model and app usage related to ads. Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through the ads SDK. Requests about that data should be directed to Google:

  • Google’s privacy policy
  • How Google uses data from apps

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Withdraw or change ad-personalization consent at any time, as described above.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored game data.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted at this address, with the date at the top changed to match.
