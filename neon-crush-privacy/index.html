Privacy policy — Neon Crush

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Neon Crush (“the app”) is published by Six Strokes Studio (Andrii Honcharuk), sixstrokesstudio@gmail.com. This policy covers the Neon Crush mobile app (Android package com.sixstrokes.neoncrush).

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your device: unlocked levels, stars and best scores, boosters, lives and life refills, your daily-reward streak, purchases you have made, and your music, sound and privacy settings. The app has no account system and does not send your progress anywhere.

Uninstalling the app removes this data. Because none of it leaves your device, we hold no copy of it and there is nothing on our side to export or delete.

Gameplay analytics (optional)

When you first open the app it asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay data. Nothing is collected unless you choose “Sure”. If you agree, the app uses Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) to record how the game is played so we can balance it, for example:

  • levels started, completed, failed or left, with score, moves left, time played and attempt number;
  • boosters used, continues, and whether optional reward ads were watched;
  • when the shop is opened and whether a purchase succeeded (the product, never payment details);
  • daily rewards claimed and running out of lives.

Unity also receives a random installation identifier generated by the app, and basic technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, country (derived from the IP address) and crash diagnostics. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

You can stop analytics at any time in Help → Analytics, where you can also choose Delete my data: the app asks Unity to delete the data it has collected for your installation and stops collecting. Unity processes this data on our behalf:

  • Unity’s privacy policy for game players

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (“Watch an ad” for +5 moves, an extra life or double loot). To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising identifier, IP address, device model and app usage related to ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested; you can change that choice later under Help → Privacy options or in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through the ads SDK. Requests about that data should be directed to Google:

  • Google’s privacy policy
  • How Google uses data from apps

In-app purchases

Purchases (boosters, lives, the starter pack and removing ads) are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the app only receives confirmation of what was bought, with a transaction identifier, so it can deliver the item on your device once.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us at the address below and we will act on it.

Your choices

  • Turn gameplay analytics on or off, or delete your analytics data, in Help → Analytics.
  • Change ad-personalization consent in Help → Privacy options (where available) or in your device settings.
  • Reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; buying “No Ads” or the starter pack removes the ads between levels.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored game data.

Changes
